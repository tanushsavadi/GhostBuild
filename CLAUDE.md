# Open Palm (branch test_sim) — project rules
Solo hackathon MVP on Even G2. Deadline Sun 2026-10-18 12:00 PDT. Live-demo reliability > features. **docs/OPEN_PALM_PLAN.md is the only execution authority on this branch.** The rest of docs/ describes GhostBuild and is historical; read it only for G2 platform notes.
- Product: a hearing G2 wearer says an English phrase; the glasses show one card per sign (ASL-LEX handshape image + two short how-to lines); the wearer signs it to a Deaf person. Scope is individual signs, not ASL translation. Never call it translation or an interpreter.
- Signs come only from g2/src/deck.json (hand-written lines, wins) and g2/src/asllex.json (every ASL-LEX 2.0 sign, built by tools/build_deck.py: hand-written lines from tools/howto.json where present, else lines composed from its phonology coding). g2/src/matcher.ts (pure, runs on the phone) turns a phrase into cards; known sentences in g2/src/phrases.json come first (ASL word order + face cue, AI-drafted, UNCHECKED). Words in neither are fingerspelled, or explained by AI only behind the AI flag and labelled AI · UNCHECKED. Never show a confident sign that is not in those files.
- Card wording: write palm direction in full ("palm facing up/down/out", "palm facing you"), never "palm up"; two lines of at most 36 characters.
- Card labels: REF-CHECKED (a person compared it with the reference video), UNCHECKED (hand-written in deck.json or howto.json, not yet compared), AUTO · UNCHECKED (generated from ASL-LEX coding), AI · UNCHECKED, FINGERSPELL · UNCHECKED. No card claims a Deaf signer checked it.
- Data: ASL-LEX 2.0 database, visualization and handshape images are CC BY-NC 4.0: non-commercial use only, credit "ASL-LEX 2.0 (asl-lex.org), CC BY-NC 4.0" in the README and on the phone page. ASL-LEX videos are © with no licence: link or view only, never bundle. Letters M and N: wpclipart.com drawings, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons. Full sign descriptions (g2/src/wiktionary.json, tools/fetch_wiktionary.py): Wiktionary, CC BY-SA 4.0, credited with a link per sign; anything derived from them stays CC BY-SA. Lifeprint/ASL University forbids use in apps: link only, never copy its text. No WLASL or How2Sign media.
- Privacy: no audio is stored by default; any tool that logs audio must be opt-in.
- Even G2: SDK 0.0.16. Verify every call with the everything-evenhub skills and node_modules/@evenrealities/even_hub_sdk/dist/index.d.ts (the .d.ts wins). updateImageRawData takes encoded PNG bytes, serially, one in flight. Images ≤ 288×144, 4-bit grey. Double-tap always exits. No API keys in g2/.
- Speech: offline Vosk on the laptop, grammar limited to the deck vocabulary plus the configured name; a typed phrase on the phone is the backup.
- Claude API: official `anthropic` SDK + the claude-api skill; claude-opus-5-5; structured outputs, not forced tool_choice. Build time for the deck; at runtime only behind the AI flag.
- Test on the physical G2 before calling anything done: the simulator has no BLE timing and renders glyphs differently.
